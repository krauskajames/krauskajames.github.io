# Practice sheet checklist, against the 2026 ALTA/NSPS standards

A plain-language checklist for the practice drawing, organized the way the 2026 Minimum Standard Detail Requirements for ALTA/NSPS Land Title Surveys are organized (effective February 23, 2026). It is a study aid in my own words, not the standard. Read the real text before relying on any item: <https://altalandsurvey.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-ALTA-NSPS-Standards.pdf> (official copies come from ALTA and NSPS).

Each item is marked:

- **Kit** – can be shown from this kit's record courses and simulated field file
- **Needs records** – needs a title commitment or recorded documents this kit does not have
- **Needs fieldwork** – can only come from a real site visit; show a simulated stand-in and label it, or leave it off and say why

## Before drawing

- [ ] The request names the 2026 standards and lists which optional Table A items are wanted. For practice: write a one-line "request" at the top of your notes choosing your Table A items. **Kit**
- [ ] Records research: the record description, adjoining documents and easements of record. Only the CSM draft is in hand. **Needs records**
- [ ] Precision: the standard's limit is 2 cm (0.07 ft) plus 50 parts per million between corners, at 95% confidence. For practice: report the traverse closure and note that a real survey tests relative positional precision, which closure alone does not prove. **Kit**

## Fieldwork (Section 5)

- [ ] Monuments found and set, with type and size, at the corners (points 101–108). **Kit, simulated**
- [ ] Rights of way and access: frontage on S. Moorland Road, the drive entrance, and Interstate 43 along the north line. **Kit for the names; widths need records**
- [ ] Lines of possession and improvements near the boundary: the fence along the Interstate 43 line (points 501–509), and anything that crosses a line. **Kit, simulated**
- [ ] Buildings (points 201–204). **Kit, simulated**
- [ ] Evidence of easements and utilities seen on the ground: manholes, catch basins, hydrant, valve, light poles. **Kit, simulated**
- [ ] Cemeteries and burial grounds: none in the kit. State that none were observed. **Needs fieldwork**
- [ ] Water features: none in the kit. State that none were observed. **Needs fieldwork**

## The sheet (Section 6)

- [ ] Everything located in the field is shown, with a legend for symbols and abbreviations. **Kit**
- [ ] The boundary with bearings and distances on every line, record values where they differ from measured, and the point of beginning tied to the section corner by the two tie courses. **Kit**
- [ ] The record description printed on the sheet, the area, and a statement of the closure. **Kit**
- [ ] Found-versus-record differences at monuments noted where they matter. **Kit, simulated**
- [ ] Recorded easements and other documents listed and plotted, or noted as not plottable. **Needs records**
- [ ] Presentation: north arrow, scale and bar scale, basis of bearings, vicinity map, sheet numbers, dates of fieldwork and of the drawing, and the preparer's name. For practice: the title block reads "Practice exercise, not a survey", with no seal and no certification. **Kit**

## Certification (Section 7)

- [ ] Do not write one. The standard's certification is signed and sealed by a licensed surveyor. On the practice sheet, put a note where it would go: "No certification: practice exercise by James Krauska, not a survey."

## Optional items (Table A)

Pick the ones a client would typically ask for on a commercial lot and try them:

| Item | What it asks for | Practice status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monuments at the major corners | Kit, simulated |
| 2 | Street address | Look it up; public |
| 3 | Flood zone | Look up the FEMA map; public |
| 4 | Gross land area | Kit |
| 5 | Contours and vertical relief | Kit, simulated surface |
| 6 | Zoning, from a report the client supplies | Needs records |
| 7 | Building dimensions, footprint and height | Kit for footprint, simulated |
| 8 | Substantial features such as parking and signs | Kit, simulated |
| 9 | Parking space count and striping | Not in the kit |
| 10 | Party wall relationships | Not applicable |
| 11 | Underground utilities from plans or locates | Surface evidence only, simulated |
| 12 | Government agency requirements | Not applicable |
| 13 | Adjoining owners from tax records | Look up; public |
| 14 | Distance to the nearest intersecting street | Measure from public mapping |
| 15 | Imagery used for some features | Possible with public orthophotos |
| 16 | Evidence of recent earthwork or construction | Needs fieldwork |
| 17 | Proposed right-of-way changes | Needs records |
| 18 | Offsite easements that benefit the property | Needs records |
| 19 | Professional liability insurance amount | Not applicable |
| 20 | Table of conditions and possible encroachments | Kit, from the fence and monument differences |
| 21 | Anything else agreed with the client | Blank |

Good practice set: items 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 13 and 20.
