Lake Winnebago from Space

My grandmother said the west wind blew the algae to our side of the lake. Ten summers of satellite images, checked against lab samples and a USGS boat survey, say she was right: after a west wind the east shore had more chlorophyll on 80% of days.

Data
Sentinel-2, Landsat, ERA5-Land, Wisconsin DNR, USGS
