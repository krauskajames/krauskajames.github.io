Land records · NG911 · PythonLive

Parcel & NG911 Data QA Toolkit

Nineteen checks run on all of Sheboygan County's public land records: 63,391 parcels, 61,811 address points and 9,852 road segments, in Python and again as PostGIS SQL, with the same 1,323 findings both ways. Overlaps, gaps, broken road links and house numbers outside their road's range, on an interactive map, plus five parcels drafted by hand from their legal descriptions and checked against the county's map.

Data
Sheboygan County parcels, address points, road centerlines
Tools
Python, geopandas, ArcGIS REST API, Leaflet

Pollinator Habitat Explorer

An interactive map of statewide habitat suitability for the host plants of the monarch, the Karner blue butterfly and a native oil-collecting bee. MaxEnt models built from 1,269 thinned iNaturalist records and 22 climate, soil and land-cover predictors, with a target-group correction for observer bias.

Data
GBIF / iNaturalist, WorldClim, SoilGrids, WISCLAND 2.0
Tools
MaxEnt, Python, R, Leaflet

3D capture · Drone · Web viewerLive

Drone 3D Captures of Milwaukee

Four interactive Gaussian splats built from drone video and viewable from any angle in the browser: the North Point Water Tower, the whole St. Mary's Hospital campus, the Milwaukee Art Museum and The Couture. It's the workflow I'm building at PX Design for client buildings: flight logs checked for coverage, cameras solved in COLMAP, splats trained in Brush, then cleaned, leveled and compressed to 2–14 MB for the web.

Data
DJI drone video and flight logs, handheld photos
Tools
ffmpeg, COLMAP, Brush, SuperSplat, three.js, Spark

About

Field-trained, code-minded

I'm a geospatial scientist who likes the whole path from a muddy preserve to a clean dataset to a tool other people can use.

At PX Design I read architectural floor plans, turn them into accurate 2D and 3D models, and build the software that speeds that work up. Before that, I mapped trails, infrastructure and invasive species across a Wisconsin land trust's preserves with a Trimble GPS and ArcGIS Field Maps.

Before GIS, I spent two years as a licensed massage therapist. It taught me the part of the job no software does: listening carefully to what someone actually needs, then delivering it on time.

I graduated from UW–Stevens Point with a 3.74 GPA. I'm most interested in public-sector and conservation GIS: land records, data quality, and automating the routine so analysts can spend time on the hard problems.
