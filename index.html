DuctBuilder

A SketchUp Ruby extension that turns traced centerlines into finished 2D or 3D walls and HVAC ductwork, with doors, windows, diffusers and VAV boxes placed in one click. A complex floor plan with HVAC now takes under 20 minutes instead of more than a week, so one person does the work of five.

Tools
Ruby, SketchUp API, JSON component library
Scope
16+ commercial and institutional buildings

Web map · Live dataLive

Starcast

A web map of stargazing conditions for anywhere on Earth: cloud, seeing, transparency, jet stream and aurora forecasts on a turning globe, plus what your own telescope can see tonight. A scheduled pipeline turns NOAA's global forecast into small files every 6 hours.

Data
NOAA GFS, Open-Meteo, NOAA SWPC, OpenStreetMap
Tools
JavaScript, D3, Python, GitHub Actions, PostGIS

About

Field-trained, code-minded

I'm a geospatial scientist who likes the whole path from a muddy preserve to a clean dataset to a tool other people can use.

At PX Design I read architectural floor plans, turn them into accurate 2D and 3D models, and build the software that speeds that work up. Before that, I mapped trails, infrastructure and invasive species across a Wisconsin land trust's preserves with a Trimble GPS and ArcGIS Field Maps.

I graduated from UW–Stevens Point with a 3.8 GPA. I'm most interested in public-sector and conservation GIS: land records, data quality, and automating the routine so analysts can spend time on the hard problems.
