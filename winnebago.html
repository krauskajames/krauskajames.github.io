Method
  1. Population. Census counts 1970–2020 and the Wisconsin DOA's final January 1 estimates through 2026, for the county and each municipality. The Village of Fox Crossing's history before 2016 is the Town of Menasha, from which it incorporated.
  2. Projections. The DOA's Vintage 2024 cohort-component projections to 2050, shown next to my own extrapolation model: linear, geometric and parabolic curves, plus modified exponential, Gompertz and logistic curves, each with an internally calculated limit and with an assumed upper limit.
  3. Economic base. BEA total employment by NAICS sector (CAEMP25N, 2012 and 2022). Location quotients against the U.S. and Wisconsin; basic jobs as each sector's employment above its national share; the base multiplier as total jobs divided by basic jobs; and a three-part shift-share against the U.S.
  4. Commuting. LEHD origin–destination blocks aggregated to counties and to municipalities through the LODES geography crosswalk.
  5. Housing and income. 2020–2024 American Community Survey 5-year estimates through the Census Data API.
  6. Map. TIGERweb county subdivisions with Lake Winnebago, Lake Butte des Morts and Lake Poygan cut out, simplified for the web and drawn with Leaflet.

Reading the results honestly

  • Small towns, wide margins. ACS figures for towns under about 3,000 people carry large margins of error. Where a median income's coefficient of variation passes 12%, the panel flags it.
  • Extrapolation is a check, not a forecast. Curves fitted to three censuses know nothing about births, deaths or migration. They are useful for asking whether the DOA's projected decline is a break from recent trends, which it is.
  • The DOA projection was made before the latest estimates. Vintage 2024 projects a slow decline from 2020, but the county has grown each year since 2022 on net in-migration.
  • Split municipalities. Appleton, Menasha and Fox Crossing extend into neighboring counties. Only the Winnebago County portion is shown.
  • BEA counts jobs, not people. A worker with two jobs counts twice, and proprietors are included, so BEA totals run higher than LODES or ACS counts.
My role

I chose the measures and methods, carried my GEOG 330 extrapolation and economic-base spreadsheets over to Winnebago County, and checked the outputs against the source tables. The data pipeline and web page were written with AI-assisted coding (Claude).

The pipeline scripts rebuild every number on this page from the original downloads.
